# Adventurer equipment artwork

Original transparent raster illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final WebP files are used by the equipment board, item detail, and inventory grid. Existing equipment values, drops, and save migration are unchanged.

Shared prompt: transparent fantasy RPG equipment icon; warm dark brown clean hand drawn outlines, flat cel-shaded colors, chunky silhouette, friendly pop Japanese fantasy style; matte color blocks, no airbrush, no realistic 3D, no text, no border or UI card. Centered, readable at 64px. Common starter gear rather than elaborate epic items.

- sword: humble straight intact steel adventurer sword, brass guard, leather grip.
- circlet: bronze circlet with one small teal gem.
- cloak: worn brown cape, ragged hem, simple clasp.
- charm: carved wooden leaf pendant on a cord.
- gloves: brown leather fingerless gloves, wrist straps.
- boots: short brown leather boots, rounded toes, folded cuffs.
- lid: dented grey cooking-pot lid, wooden center handle.
- adventurer: fully clothed adult male dog adventurer, plush stocky build, broad shoulders, floppy ears, golden cream fur, teal tunic, belted shorts and shoulder satchel; relaxed hands-on-hips full-body pose.

The portrait is static; changing equipment updates the surrounding icons and stats rather than clothing layers on the portrait.
